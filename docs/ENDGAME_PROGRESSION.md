# Gone Rogue: Endgame Progression & Replayability

## The Uber System (Replay Difficulty)

To separate biome progression from global difficulty, we have transitioned the AWOL selector to use the **Uber System**. 

| Uber Level | Label | Multipliers | Unlock Condition |
|------------|-------|-------------|------------------|
| Uber 0     | Standard | 1.0x Stats | Default |
| Uber 1     | Elite | 1.3x Stats | Complete Uber 0 (Beat Floor 30) |
| Uber 2     | Hell | 1.6x Stats | Complete Uber 1 (Beat Floor 30) |

**Note**: "Biome Tiers" still refer to the floor ranges (T1: 1-10, T2: 11-22, T3: 23-30) encountered during a single run.

## Roadmap: Making Replays Interesting

The following TODOs are planned to enhance the "Uber" experience beyond just stat scaling:

### TODO: Uber-Specific Affixes
- Introduce enemy affixes that only appear in Uber 1+.
- **Uber 1 (Elite)**: Enemies can spawn with "Vengeful" (explodes on death) or "Regen" (heals 5% per turn).
- **Uber 2 (Hell)**: Enemies can spawn with "Void-Touched" (teleports on hit) or "Shrouded" (stealth for 1 turn after spawning).

### TODO: Unique Rewards
- Successful extraction on Uber 1+ grants "Uber Scraps" used for permanent card upgrades.
- Uber 2 completion unlocks the "Ghost" avatar with unique stealth capabilities.

### TODO: Environmental Hazards
- Uber 1+: Random floor tiles can become "Unstable" (damages player on entry).
- Uber 2+: Map-wide "Darkness" effect reduces base sight range by 2.

### TODO: Boss Variations
- Bosses on Uber 1+ should have a "Second Phase" triggered at 30% HP.
