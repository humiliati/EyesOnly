OPERATION KANIKSU ECLIPSE
Live Action Spy Game Bible — 12-20 Players | 4-10 Hours | City-Wide Deployment

SECTION A: GAME OVERVIEW & THEATRE FRAMEWORK
A.1 Concept Statement
Operation KaniksU Eclipse is a hybrid live-action/multiplayer ARG that transforms a city into an active intelligence battlefield. Players assume the roles of field agents from a fictional shadow agency (THE FALCON INITIATIVE) who must locate, identify, and extract a high-value Chinese asset (CHEN WEI-LIN) while hunting a suspected mole within their own ranks. The game unfolds across real city locations, using physical geocaches, cryptographic challenges, and live actor interactions to progress a narrative that mirrors the ARG's themes of ancient conspiracies, institutional betrayal, and the moral weight of truth.
The game operates on a tension clock: players have 4-10 hours (configurable) before the Chinese asset is either extracted by the player's team, captured by hostile forces (Pinkertons), or lost entirely. Within that window, players must navigate rivalries, decode encrypted transmissions, participate in emergency JTX (Jump Team Extraction) meetings where traitors may be voted out, and ultimately decide whether to expose the truth about the pandas or preserve the balance of global power.
A.2 The Opening Theatre: "The Hollow Creek Brief"
A.2.1 Pre-Game Setup (30-45 minutes before game start)
Players arrive at a designated "safehouse" location (typically a bar, community center, or private event space with theatrical capability). They are greeted by a civilian actor playing a WAITRESS or BARTENDER who offers them a "special drink menu." Each drink corresponds to a role assignment:
•	Old Fashioned → Field Operative (Blue Team)
•	Negroni → Communications Specialist (Blue Team)
•	Whiskey Sour → Intelligence Analyst (Blue Team)
•	Mojito → Logistics Coordinator (Blue Team)
•	Gin and Tonic → Security Specialist (Blue Team)
•	Vodka Martini (shaken, not stirred) → Deep Cover Asset (Unknown Allegiance)
•	Water → Suspected Mole (Red Team)
The drinks are served with cocktail napkins containing their first mission packet: a folded document marked "CLASSIFIED — FALCON EYES ONLY" that contains their initial briefing and a physical geocache coordinate written in invisible UV ink (revealed under blacklight at the bar).
A.2.2 The Briefing Theatre (15-20 minutes)
Once all players have arrived and received their drinks, the lights dim. A PROJECTION SCREEN displays static, then resolves into a video transmission from "DIRECTOR MORROW" (pre-recorded by an actor, 3-4 minutes long):
VIDEO SCRIPT:
"Good evening, Agents. You're receiving this briefing because Operation Kaniksu has reached a critical inflection point. For forty years, the Falcon Initiative has monitored a network we've codenamed 'Kaniksu' — an ancient alliance between Jesuit institutions and private intelligence forces that has shaped global events since the 17th century."
"Three weeks ago, our asset inside Kaniksu went silent. Chen Wei-Lin. Chinese national. American citizen since 2015. His father, Chen Wei-Lin Sr., was a geneticist who worked on a classified project in Hollow Creek, Idaho — a project involving panda tissue engineering that has implications for global biological security that I cannot overstate."
"Chen Jr. was supposed to extract himself from Kaniksu last month. Instead, he vanished. We have reason to believe he's still in the city, but we don't know whose control he's under. The Jesuits? The Pinkertons? Or his own?"
"What's worse: our last transmission from field Agent Petra Kozlov suggests that the mole we suspected three years ago has become active again. Someone in this room — or someone you're about to meet — is feeding information to Kaniksu."
"Your mission: locate Chen Wei-Lin. Determine his status and allegiance. Extract him if possible. Identify the mole if you can. And above all: do not let the truth about Hollow Creek become public. The consequences of premature disclosure would destabilize three governments and end the Falcon Initiative as we know it."
"You have ten hours until the Pinkerton private security forces arrive in force. Time is short. Trust no one. And good hunting."
The screen cuts to black. When the lights return, the WAITRESS/BARTENDER has left a SECOND drink at each place setting — this one is a SHOT with a paper umbrella. The umbrella contains a QR code leading to the first digital puzzle, and the napkin beneath it contains the first geocache coordinate (already revealed by the UV light on their first napkin).

A.3 Role Distribution Matrix
A.3.1 Blue Team: The Falcon Initiative (15-18 players, depending on total count)
ROLE	COUNT	SPECIALTY	STARTING EQUIPMENT
Field Operative	4-6	Movement, search, confrontation	Handcuffs, tactical vest with "FALCON" branding, flashlight
Communications Specialist	2-3	Codec operation, signal interception	Walkie-talkie (half-duplex), frequency scanner, coded message pad
Intelligence Analyst	2-3	Pattern recognition, evidence synthesis	Magnifying glass, evidence bag, dossier folder
Logistics Coordinator	2-3	Resource acquisition, safehouse access	Master keycard (5 locations), budget envelope ($500 play money)
Security Specialist	2-3	Defense, locks, surveillance detection	Lockpick set, camera detector, defensive flashlight
Deep Cover Asset	1-2	Unknown — may be Blue, Red, or Independent	No identification, civilian clothing, hidden microdot
Blue Team Win Conditions:
1.	Successfully extract Chen Wei-Lin alive AND identify/vote out the mole before Pinkerton forces arrive
2.	Or: Discover the truth about Hollow Creek and decide collectively to expose or conceal it
A.3.2 Red Team: The Kaniksu Network (5-7 players, hidden identities)
ROLE	COUNT	FUNCTION
Jesuit Handler	1	Guides Red Team movements, activates "dead drops," can call one emergency JTX
Pinkerton Operative	1-2	Surveillance, interception, can "arrest" one Blue Team member per hour
Sleeper Agent	2-3	Appears Blue Team, activated by Jesuit Handler at critical moments
Red Team Win Conditions:
1.	Prevent Chen Wei-Lin extraction (capture him for Kaniksu)
2.	Ensure the mole remains undiscovered until extraction window closes
3.	If mole is exposed, ensure at least one Red Team member survives to continue the mission
A.3.3 The Asset: Chen Wei-Lin (Player - usually AI controlled or played by actor)
Chen Wei-Lin is not played by a human in most configurations. He appears at geocache locations as a "recording" (audio/video message), a written letter, or as an actor who can only speak in riddles. The exception is if a player role-cards as Chen (advanced configuration), in which case they are escorted by two Blue Team actors and have limited agency.
Chen's Status: The truth about Chen is layered. He genuinely wants to expose the panda conspiracy, but he's been manipulated by both Kaniksu (who promise to protect his family) and the Falcon Initiative (who promise amnesty). His actual location is only revealed when the final puzzle is solved.

SECTION B: ACT STRUCTURE & TIMELINE
B.1 Game Clock Overview
The game operates on a 4-10 hour timeline divided into four Acts. The default configuration is 6 hours, but can be scaled by the Director.
ACT	DURATION	FOCUS	JTX TRIGGERS
ACT 1: THE HUNT	90-120 min	Geocache search, evidence collection	2 available
ACT 2: THE IDENTIFICATION	90-120 min	Cryptography gates, mole accusations	2 available
ACT 3: THE EXTRACTION	60-90 min	Final chase, JTX showdown	1 available
ACT 4: THE RESOLUTION	30-45 min	Final choice, ending sequence	N/A
The Pinkerton Clock: At the 5-hour mark (or 75% through total time), Pinkerton forces "arrive." This triggers a "lockdown" phase where certain geocache locations become inaccessible, Blue Team movement is restricted, and the final extraction window narrows to 60 minutes.

SECTION C: ACT 1 — THE HUNT (90-120 minutes)
C.1 Opening Sequence: "The First Drop"
Once players decode their first coordinate (a simple substitution cipher on the cocktail napkin), they discover a PHYSICAL GEOCACHE hidden within 200 meters of the safehouse location. The cache contains:
•	1x Evidence Packet #1: Contains a partial personnel file (redacted) showing a Falcon Initiative agent who "voluntarily resigned" in 2019. The photo is obscured, but the name is partially visible: "-ARTINAS."
•	1x Cryptographic Key A: A printed QR code that, when scanned, reveals the next geocache coordinate.
•	1x Audio Recording: A 30-second clip of a woman speaking in Mandarin with English subtitles: "The bamboo remembers. The children of the Middle Kingdom are not what they seem. Find the shrine before the pink moon."
•	1x Actor Interaction Card: "Meet 'The Librarian' at Central Library within 60 minutes. Show this card. Ask about the 1987 acquisition records."
Narrative Function: This first drop establishes three core mysteries: the agent who resigned (potential mole), the "bamboo" reference (panda connection), and the "pink moon" (timing clue for the Pinkerton arrival).
C.2 Geocache Network (6-8 Locations)
The game deploys 6-8 physical geocaches across the play area. Each contains Evidence Packets, Cryptographic Keys, and occasionally Actor Interaction Cards. Locations should be publicly accessible spaces (parks, libraries, coffee shops, public plazas) with permission from local authorities if required.
Geocache 1: "The Shrine" (Central Park or equivalent)
Location Clue (from Audio Recording): "The shrine stands where four paths meet beneath the iron master's gaze."
Contents:
•	Evidence Packet #2: A 1987 newspaper clipping about "Rare Pandas Arrive at Hollow Creek Zoo" with a photograph showing a Jesuit priest standing beside the first pandas.
•	Actor Interaction Card: "Speak to 'Father Mathias' at St. Sebastian Church. He has the baptismal records you need."
Narrative Function: This is the player's first concrete evidence of the Jesuit-panda connection. The newspaper clipping establishes the 1987 timeline, and Father Mathias is the first actor encounter.
Geocache 2: "The Ledger" (Downtown Office Building or business district)
Location Clue (from Evidence Packet #2): "Follow the money to the tower that never sleeps."
Contents:
•	Evidence Packet #3: Financial records showing monthly payments of $50,000 to "Asset CW-7" from a holding company traced to the Pinkerton Family Trust.
•	Cryptographic Key B: A Vigenère cipher wheel (physical or printed) needed to decode later messages.
•	Actor Interaction Card: "The 'Archivist' at the Historical Society can help you read between the lines. Show this card. Ask about the 1947 donation."
Narrative Function: This introduces the Pinkertons as a financial power and establishes Chen as a paid asset (not just a volunteer). The Archivist actor will provide context about the Jesuits' long presence in the city.
Geocache 3: "The Laboratory" (University or medical building area)
Location Clue (from Cryptographic Key A decoded): "Where science meets the sacred, the children sleep in glass."
Contents:
•	Evidence Packet #4: Research notes from "Project Chimera" — redacted heavily but showing phrases like "stem cell integration," "opossum host species," and "organ viability for mature recipients."
•	1x "Specimen Photograph": A grainy black-and-white image of what appears to be a panda with human-like hands.
•	Actor Interaction Card: "Dr. Hoffmann at the clinic has been treating the 'secret patients.' Show this card. Ask about his 2019 license suspension."
Narrative Function: This is the "chimera reveal" — players are discovering that the pandas are not natural animals but engineered organisms. The Dr. Hoffmann actor can confirm or deny the research, creating uncertainty.
Geocache 4: "The Vault" (Bank or financial district)
Location Clue (from Cryptographic Key B decoded): "Behind the iron door, the family seal waits for its brother."
Contents:
•	Evidence Packet #5: A safe deposit box key (physical prop) and a note: "For when the ring returns."
•	A PINKERTON COIN (prop): Identical to the one in the ARG, showing the Pinkerton private eye insignia.
•	Actor Interaction Card: "The 'Banker' knows which boxes were accessed this week. Show this card. Ask about the Jesuit client."
Narrative Function: This connects the Jesuit Ring (from the ARG) to the Pinkerton Signet — the two ancient symbols meeting. The Banker actor can reveal which faction accessed the vault recently.
Geocache 5: "The Signal" (Radio tower, hilltop, or high point)
Location Clue (from Evidence Packet #3): "Listen for the voice that travels farther than thought."
Contents:
•	Evidence Packet #6: A frequency chart showing the codecs used by different factions (Falcon Initiative: 141.3, Jesuit Network: 144.4, Pinkerton Main: 143.3, Russian Contact: 142.7).
•	1x Audio Recording: Chen Wei-Lin's voice: "I know you're looking for me. But first, you must understand what my father understood: the truth is a weapon, and someone has to decide if it's worth firing."
•	Cryptographic Key C: A one-time pad sheet for the final message decryption.
Narrative Function: This is the first direct contact from Chen. The frequency chart gives players the tools to "call" different contacts via the Codec system (a phone app or physical radio set).
Geocache 6: "The Witness" (Residential area, church, or cemetery)
Location Clue (from Actor Interaction Cards collected): "Where the silent speak and the dead remember."
Contents:
•	Evidence Packet #7: A death certificate for "Chen Wei-Lin Sr." dated 1987, but a photograph showing the same man alive in 2015.
•	1x Photograph: Chen Wei-Lin Sr. holding a baby panda, with the inscription on back: "For my son. When you're ready to understand."
•	Actor Interaction Card: "The 'Ghost' at the cemetery has been waiting for you. Show this card. Ask about the 1987 fire."
Narrative Function: This reveals that Chen's father may not have died — or was "replaced" by something else. The Ghost actor (dressed as a groundskeeper or historian) provides the final piece of the timeline.
C.3 Actor Encounter System
The game includes 5-7 live actors who appear at designated locations. Actors have scripted knowledge but can improvise within bounds. They may confirm, deny, or complicate the evidence players have collected.
Actor 1: "Father Mathias" — The Jesuit Handler
Location: St. Sebastian Church or equivalent Costume: Black clergy shirt, simple cross, reading glasses Knowledge: Can confirm Jesuit presence in the city since 1860s, knows about "the agreement" with the Pinkertons, can discuss the "shrine" but claims ignorance about pandas. Willing to help if players demonstrate they've found evidence of the conspiracy. Scripted Response to Evidence Packet #2: "The church has been here a long time. We've seen empires rise and fall. The pandas... yes, the bishop at the time was involved. But it wasn't what you think. We were protecting something. Or someone." Reveal Trigger: If players show the Jesuit Ring (from Geocache 4 key), Mathias will reveal: "The Ring has chosen. That hasn't happened in two hundred years. Perhaps... perhaps you are the one."
Actor 2: "The Archivist" — The Historical Society Gatekeeper
Location: Local Historical Society or library archive room Costume: Cardigan, reading glasses, name tag reading "Volunteer" Knowledge: Extensive knowledge of local history, including the 1947 "Pinkerton donation" that built the research wing of the local hospital. Has records of every Jesuit priest assigned to the city since 1850. Scripted Response to 1947 Donation: "The Pinkertons donated the land for St. Sebastian's expansion. It's in the records. But there's an unusual clause: the Pinkertons retain 'access rights to the basement archives.' I've never seen anyone use them." Reveal Trigger: If players ask about the basement archives, the Archivist will say: "I found something in the 2019 renovation records. The basement was redesigned. Expanded. There are tunnels that don't appear on any city map."
Actor 3: "Dr. Hoffmann" — The Complicit Physician
Location: Medical clinic or pharmacy (in character as a doctor) Costume: White coat, stethoscope, nervous demeanor Knowledge: Treated "special patients" from the research program. Can confirm organ transplant procedures but claims he didn't know the source of the organs. Knows about the 2019 license suspension but refuses to discuss it. Scripted Response to 2019 Suspension: "There were... complications. Patients who shouldn't have survived. I didn't create them. I just... treated them." Reveal Trigger: If players show Evidence Packet #4 (Research Notes), Hoffmann will break: "The pandas aren't animals. They never were. They're... they're vessels. The organs grow inside them. For us. For people who can afford forever."
Actor 4: "The Banker" — The Financial Gatekeeper
Location: Bank lobby or financial district (in character as a teller) Costume: Professional attire, friendly but guarded Knowledge: Processes "special accounts" for high-net-worth clients. Has seen the Pinkerton family make regular deposits to a numbered account since 1987. Saw a Jesuit priest access a safety deposit box last week. Scripted Response to Jesuit Client: "Father... I'll just say Father. Very old. Very calm. He accessed Box 714. I didn't see what he took, but he smiled when he left. Like he was carrying something important." Reveal Trigger: If players show the Pinkerton Coin, the Banker will whisper: "The Pinkertons have a man. He comes every month. Different name each time. But I've seen his face in the newspaper. He's not a banker. He's something else."
Actor 5: "The Ghost" — The Cemetery Witness
Location: Cemetery or memorial park Costume: Groundskeeper uniform, old radio playing static Knowledge: Has worked at the cemetery for 40 years. Saw Chen Wei-Lin Sr. "die" in 1987, but saw the same man alive in 2015. Knows about the 1987 fire at the research facility. Scripted Response to 1987 Fire: "I was a kid. The whole hill went up in flames. They said it was an electrical fire. But I saw the smoke. It was green. Like the northern lights, but wrong." Reveal Trigger: If players show Chen Sr.'s photograph, the Ghost will sit down: "That man. I planted roses on his grave in 1987. And I served him coffee at the diner in 2015. Either God made a mistake, or someone else did."
C.4 ACT 1 JTX (Jump Team Extraction) Emergency Meeting
Trigger: Players may call ONE JTX at any point during Act 1 by finding a "JTX Activation Card" in any geocache or by having 3+ players simultaneously at the same location and using their radios to broadcast "JTX JTX JTX."
Procedure:
1.	All players must gather at a designated "Extraction Point" (usually the safehouse or a central location announced by the Director).
2.	The Director (or a narrator actor) reads the JTX preamble: "All agents, this is a Jump Team Extraction emergency meeting. We have detected a potential security breach. Stand by for identification protocols."
3.	Each player is given a BALLOT CARD and must write down:
•	Who they believe the mole is (if any)
•	One piece of evidence they have collected
•	One player they trust completely
4.	Ballots are collected and tallied publicly.
5.	If a player receives 50%+ of "mole" votes, they are "extracted" (removed from play for 15 minutes while being "interrogated").
6.	During the 15-minute extraction, the accused must answer three questions from the group. After 15 minutes, the group votes AGAIN:
•	If the majority still believes the accused is guilty, they are removed from the game permanently (they become a "spectator" and may have secret information to share).
•	If the majority believes the accused is innocent, the accusers each lose 10% of their "trust score" (tracked by the Director).
Narrative Function: The JTX creates social tension and forces players to make judgments with incomplete information. The mole (Red Team) may strategically accuse innocent players to create confusion, while Blue Team players must weigh evidence against gut instinct.

SECTION D: ACT 2 — THE IDENTIFICATION (90-120 minutes)
D.1 Mid-Game Crisis: "The Pinkerton Arrival Warning"
At the midpoint of the game (approximately 3 hours in), all players receive an audio broadcast (via the game's Discord server, a text message blast, or a live actor delivery):
BROADCAST SCRIPT:
"Attention Falcon Initiative assets. This is Director Morrow. We have a situation."
"Pinkerton private security forces have been mobilized. They have acquired intelligence suggesting our operation in the city. We estimate their arrival window is now 3-4 hours."
"If Chen Wei-Lin is not located and extracted within that window, Pinkerton teams will secure the city and our operation will be compromised."
"Additionally: Our internal security has flagged a transmission anomaly. Someone in your network is communicating with an external frequency. We believe the mole has made contact."
"Find Chen. Find the mole. And for God's sake, hide what you've discovered. We cannot let this become public."
"Morrow out."
Game Effect: The Pinkerton Clock begins. A countdown timer is displayed at the safehouse and via the game's digital interface. After 3 hours, Pinkerton actors (2-3 additional live actors in tactical gear) will begin "patrolling" and may "detain" players who are in restricted areas or who trigger specific security systems.
D.2 Cryptography Gate System

Gate 1: The Substitution Lock
Location: Safehouse or central location Puzzle: A Vigenère cipher wheel (from Geocache 2) must be used to decode a message hidden on the wall of the safehouse (or in a prominent local mural that players must locate).
Encrypted Message:

Decrypted Message: "THE PANDAS ARE NOT REAL, WE'LL NEVER TELL. WHEN THE CHILDREN WAKE, THE TRUTH WILL BREAK."
Reward: A COORDINATE for Geocache 7 and a FRAGMENT of Chen's location (an address fragment: "1400 block of...").
Gate 2: The Transposition Key
Location: University campus or library Puzzle: Players must arrange a set of wooden blocks (or 3D printed tiles) with letters on them to form a meaningful message. The blocks are hidden in Geocaches 3 and 5.
Block Letters: A, C, E, H, I, M, N, O, P, R, S, T
Solution: CHEMINOPARSTORCHIMERA (an anagram of "CHIMERA" with "CHEMIN OPENS ART CHI" — "the path opens through art and chi")
Alternative Solution Path: Players who solve the anagram first can skip the block arrangement.
Reward: A SECOND COORDINATE fragment ("...Eastwood Avenue, near the old radio tower") and an AUDIO CLIP from Chen: "You found the first key. The second is hidden where the students study the dead. The third... the third you must earn."
Gate 3: The One-Time Pad
Location: Radio tower or high point (Geocache 5) Puzzle: Players must use the one-time pad sheet from Geocache 6 to decode a final message. The message is:
Encrypted Message:

•	Row 23, Columns 15-42-33-11 → spells "CHEN"
•	Row 02, Columns 22-33-12-44 → spells "IS"
•	Row 41, Columns 31-22-11-03 → spells "HERE"
Decrypted Message: "CHEN IS HERE"
Reward: The final location: "The Old Radio Tower, Room 412" (or equivalent).
Narrative Function: The cryptographic gates represent the layers of secrecy surrounding Chen. Each solved puzzle reveals more of his location and reinforces the theme that truth requires effort to uncover.
D.3 The Mole Reveal Subplot
During Act 2, the game introduces additional evidence that points to a specific player as the mole. This evidence is distributed through:
1.	Actor Testimony: The Ghost, Dr. Hoffmann, or other actors may "accidentally" reveal information about a specific player: "I recognize that walk. One of your agents came to me three years ago. Offered me money to destroy records. Had a scar on his left hand."
2.	Physical Evidence: A "dropped ID badge" (prop) found in a geocache belongs to a player — suggesting that player is the mole.
3.	Digital Evidence: The game's Discord server or message board "accidentally" exposes a private message from a player to an external frequency (pre-scripted by the Director).
Mole Reveal Options:
Option A: The Accidental Confession A geocache contains a AUDIO RECORDING of a player (pre-recorded by the actor playing that player's role) discussing the mission with a "Jesuit Handler." The recording is ambiguous — it could be a genuine mole or a deep cover operative feeding disinformation.
Option B: The Forensic Analysis Evidence Packet #7 (Chen Sr.'s photograph) contains a hidden watermark that, when photographed with a specific filter app, reveals a coded message: "AGENT [PLAYER NAME] IS THE KEY. TRUST NO ONE."
Option C: The Actor's Testimony Father Mathias (if players show him evidence of the mole) will say: "I know who you're looking for. The one who came to me asking about the Ring. He has the scar. And he's in this room right now."
Narrative Function: The mole subplot creates paranoia and forces players to make difficult decisions. However, the Director should ensure that the "mole" evidence is always ambiguous enough that players might vote out an innocent person — and must prepare for all outcomes.
D.4 ACT 2 JTX (Jump Team Extraction) Emergency Meeting
Trigger: Available after players have collected at least 4 Evidence Packets.
Special Rules for Act 2 JTX:
1.	Players may now "detain" suspected moles for 30 minutes (longer interrogation).
2.	The detained player must answer: "Where were you when [EVENT X] happened?"
3.	If the detained player cannot provide an alibi verified by 2+ witnesses, they receive a "suspicion token."
4.	Three suspicion tokens = automatic extraction from the game.
Narrative Function: This raises the stakes. The mole is now actively being hunted, and Red Team players must be careful not to trip their own traps.

SECTION E: ACT 3 — THE EXTRACTION (60-90 minutes)
E.1 The Final Location: "The Radio Tower"
Once all three cryptographic gates are solved, players discover Chen Wei-Lin's location: Room 412 of the Old Radio Tower (a real building or a controlled event space). However, the path to Chen is blocked by:
1.	Pinkerton Guards: 2-3 actors in tactical gear who will "detain" any Blue Team player without proper credentials.
2.	The Jesuit Ring Gate: A physical lock that requires the Jesuit Ring (from Geocache 4 key) to open.
3.	The Mole Interference: The suspected mole (if not yet identified) may try to prevent extraction by alerting Pinkerton guards or destroying the final evidence.
E.1.1 The Jesuit Ring Gate
Setup: A physical lockbox (or door with a hasp) secured with a custom lock (or a series of combination locks). Solution: Players must have collected the Jesuit Ring from Geocache 4. The ring's inscription ("ADIUTORIUM NOSTRUM IN NOMINE DOMINI") is the combination: 5-1-4-9-20-18-15-18-18-21-13 (A=1, B=2, etc.) Alternative: If the ring was not collected, players must solve a secondary puzzle (a quote from Father Mathias recorded earlier) to "pick" the lock.
Narrative Reward: The lock opens to reveal a SECONDARY ROOM containing Chen Wei-Lin (an actor or mannequin depending on configuration) and a final audio/visual message.
E.1.2 The Chen Wei-Lin Encounter
If Chen is an actor: Chen will be bound to a chair, visibly distressed. He has two things to say:
1.	"My father didn't create the pandas. He tried to stop them. And they killed him for it. Twice."
2.	"The truth about Hollow Creek... it's not just about pandas. The Pinkertons are using the organ program to stay alive. Literally. The wealthy ones. They've been replacing their organs for sixty years."
3.	"You have a choice now. Take me to your handlers, and they'll bury everything. Or take the truth public, and watch the world burn."
Chen then asks the group: "Which will it be?"
If Chen is a recording: The message plays: "If you're hearing this, you found the path. My father used to say the truth is a weapon that doesn't discriminate. It cuts everyone. Including you. Before you decide, ask yourself: is the world ready to know? And if not... who decides?"
E.2 The Final Choice Matrix
At the climax of the game, the group must make a COLLECTIVE DECISION that determines the ending. This is modeled after the ARG's final choice but adapted for live play.
E.2.1 Decision Setup
The group gathers in the extraction room. Chen (or his recording) has presented the truth. The Pinkerton Clock shows less than 60 minutes remaining. The Director (or a narrator actor) announces:
"You have reached the final decision point. The truth about Hollow Creek, the pandas, and the Pinkerton organ program is in your hands. You must choose now: what will you do with this knowledge?"
E.2.2 Voting Procedure
Each player receives a BALLOT CARD with four options:
Option 1: EXPOSE THE TRUTH
•	Action: Use the game's social media accounts or a pre-prepared press release to broadcast the evidence publicly.
•	Consequence: The game ends immediately. All players receive a "Victory" status (Blue Team win), but the narrative outcome is "CHAOS" — markets crash, governments deny, and the players become legendary figures in the shadows.
Option 2: PRESERVE THE BALANCE
•	Action: Destroy all evidence and deliver Chen to Falcon Initiative handlers.
•	Consequence: The game ends. All players receive a "Partial Victory" — Chen is safe, the mole may or may not be identified, and the truth remains hidden. The Kaniksu Protocol continues.
Option 3: EXTRACT CHEN ONLY
•	Action: Take Chen and disappear, neither exposing the truth nor delivering it to Falcon Initiative.
•	Consequence: A "New Shadow" ending. Players split the group: some stay to face consequences, others vanish with Chen. The Director determines the outcome based on group division.
Option 4: SACRIFICE
•	Action: Trigger the self-destruct protocol (metaphorical or literal — e.g., destroy the game site) to eliminate Pinkerton forces and evidence simultaneously.
•	Consequence: A "Martyr" ending. The players succeed but at great cost. The truth is partially exposed, the Pinkerton network is crippled, and the players become legends.
E.2.3 Tally and Resolution
The votes are collected and tallied. The majority choice determines the ending. In case of a tie, the group has 5 minutes to negotiate a compromise or the Director breaks the tie based on "dramatic storytelling principles."
Ending Narratives (delivered by a narrator or video):
Ending A — The Truth Agent: "You chose to expose the truth. Within hours, the world knows about Hollow Creek, the panda chimeras, and the Pinkerton organ program. The stock market crashes. Three governments fall. And somewhere in the shadows, the survivors of the Falcon Initiative tell stories about the agents who burned it all down."
Ending B — The Guardian Choice: "You chose to preserve the balance. Chen Wei-Lin is delivered to Falcon Initiative custody. The mole is identified (or not). And the Kaniksu Protocol continues, its guardians watching and waiting for another century. You did your job. But the truth sleeps, dreaming of the day it will wake."
Ending C — The New Shadow: "You chose to disappear. Some of you walk away with Chen. Others face the consequences. The Pinkertons regroup. The Jesuits watch. And somewhere, a new generation of agents learns that some truths are too heavy for one person to carry."
Ending D — The Sacrifice: "You chose to burn it all down. The Pinkerton forces are eliminated. The facility is destroyed. And you... you become the legend that future agents will whisper about. The ones who gave everything so the world would know."
E.3 ACT 3 JTX (Jump Team Extraction) Final Meeting
Trigger: Available during the Final Choice voting period.
Special Rules:
