# Testing Floors Design Document

## 1. Overview

This document outlines the design for a set of testing floors that will be used to test all of the items, puzzles, and environmental synergies in the game. The floors will be designed to be challenging but not impossible, and they will provide a good overview of all the game's mechanics.

## 2. Goals

- To create a set of testing floors that will be used to test all of the game's mechanics.
- To ensure that the testing floors are well-balanced and provide a good player experience.
- To provide a clear and concise guide for creating the testing floors in the world-building engine.

### 3.4. Test Floor 4: "The Chain Reaction"

**Biome:** Industrial Complex

**Goal:** To teach the player about environmental chain reactions.

**Layout:**

*   A large, open room with a mix of oil, water, and electrical hazards.
*   A number of enemies patrolling the room.

**Puzzles:**

*   **Oil Fire:** The player can ignite the oil to create a fire that will spread to other oil slicks.
*   **Electrified Water:** The player can use a tazer to electrify the water and stun enemies.

**Items:**

*   Lighter
*   Tazer
*   Oil Slick card
*   Water Bottle

### 3.5. Test Floor 5: "The Boss"

**Biome:** Aerospace Museum

**Goal:** To test the player's knowledge of all the game's mechanics in a challenging boss battle.

**Layout:**

*   A large, open room with a boss enemy in the center.
*   A number of smaller enemies patrolling the room.
*   A variety of environmental hazards, including oil, water, and electrical hazards.

**Puzzles:**

*   The player must use all of their knowledge of the game's mechanics to defeat the boss.

**Items:**

*   A variety of cards, including synergy cards and game-breaking cards.
