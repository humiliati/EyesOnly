What is EyesOnly Live ARG?

EyesOnly is a live-action ARG (Alternate Reality Game) experience operated out of Sandpoint, Idaho. Players take on the role of field operatives under the direction of a scenario controller designated M.

The terminal you access at flapsandseals.com serves as your command interface. It provides mission briefings, inventory, field comms, and real-time coordination.

There are two levels of access:

  • RED PLAYER Create a free account to access Gone Rogue (standalone roguelike minigame) and all single-player terminal content. No scenario join code required.
  • ACTIVE Receive a scenario join code from M to participate in live EyesOnly ARG events. Join codes grant access to real-time field ops, M communications, escalation events, and dead drop coordination.
Joining a Live Scenario

Live scenarios are invitation-only events. To participate in a live run:

  • Obtain a 6-character join code from M or an authorized handler.
  • From the terminal, use the JOIN command with your code and callsign.
  • Once joined, the ACTIVE status will appear in your AWOL menu.
  • The [M] PING BACK button will become available for field comms.

The AWOL menu in the terminal header shows your current M /ops link status:

  • OFFLINE — Not joined to a scenario. Standalone Rogue play available.
  • ACTIVE — Scenario-joined. Live ARG features enabled.
Safety & Conduct

▲ IMPORTANT: EyesOnly is a fictional game experience. All scenarios, characters, and events are entirely made up. Any resemblance to real persons, organizations, or events is coincidental.

  • Do not trespass on private property during live field events.
  • All physical-world activities must comply with local laws.
  • If asked by law enforcement or property owners to stop or leave, do so immediately.
  • Dead drops and props are non-hazardous. Never plant anything that could be mistaken for a dangerous item.
  • Participants must be 18 years of age or older, or have guardian consent.
  • Respect other participants and members of the public at all times.
  • This is a game. Real-world harm is never acceptable and is not part of any scenario.

SAFE WORD: At any time, any participant may say or type CUTSCENE to immediately pause all in-game interactions and step out of character for any reason — no questions asked.
