EYES ONLY runs live-action spy scenarios across Sandpoint, Idaho and the surrounding Lake Pend Oreille region. Participants form teams, receive classified briefings, and complete objectives using real-world terrain, coded communications, and interactions with actors embedded throughout the community.

Local businesses serve as mission waypoints, safe houses, dead-drop locations, and NPC encounter sites. Restaurants, hotels, shops, and entertainment venues can host scenario elements that bring foot traffic directly to their doors. We handle all scenario logistics — you provide the location and a willingness to play along.

Actors play NPCs — informants, adversaries, shopkeepers with secrets, surveillance targets, and more. Volunteers assist with scenario logistics, equipment staging, and safety oversight. Both roles receive briefings, character sheets, and compensation or volunteer credits depending on the engagement type.

All participants (players, actors, and volunteers) sign liability waivers before any scenario begins. Partnership Premises-Use Agreement

This document outlines the legal framework governing the use of partner business premises during EYES ONLY live-action scenarios. By signing below, you acknowledge and agree to the following terms:

1. Premises Use: EYES ONLY may stage scenario elements (dead drops, NPC encounters, player waypoints) at your location during active scenarios with advance notice and mutual scheduling.

2. Hold Harmless: Each party agrees to hold the other harmless from claims arising from their own negligence. EYES ONLY maintains comprehensive general liability insurance and will name your business as additional insured upon request.

3. Safety: All scenario activities on your premises will comply with local fire codes, occupancy limits, and ADA requirements. EYES ONLY assigns a safety officer to each active scenario.

4. Termination: Either party may terminate this agreement with 14 days written notice. Active scenarios will be adjusted to remove your location.
